# ADR-0005: Distilled social outcome objective

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-02

## Decision

The M4 model distills bounded, structured social reactions from reviewed anchors, teacher judgments, and constrained augmentation. It is not evaluated as an autonomous discoverer of real social laws.

Success means stable directional behavior, held-out combination generalization, calibrated probabilities, hard-constraint safety, maintainability relative to hand-authored branches, local batch inference, and interesting but traceable simulation chains.

Heuristic labels provide engineering coverage and fallback; they are not the sole evidence for social quality.
